# Ox is the floor: Eta's core is an Ox-shaped library over Eio

Compared on 2026-09-29 against the direct-style structured-concurrency family (Scala Ox, Kotlin coroutines + Arrow, Java Loom, Haskell ki) and the effect-system family (ZIO, Effect TS, Koka, Kyo, Haskell effectful/polysemy), Eta's core is defined as **an Ox-shaped library over Eio**: everything on Ox's floor (https://ox.softwaremill.com) exists in Eta in direct style, nothing on the floor is skipped, and the only additions above it are ZIO's `Cause` tree and Koka's handler discipline (ADR-0006). Ox is the floor because it is what a team that had written a ZIO-style stack built after choosing direct style, on a runtime (Loom) that stands to Ox as Eio stands to Eta; its table of contents is therefore the checklist, and its two error kinds are the doctrine: unrecoverable errors are exceptions, untyped on purpose so nobody builds control flow on them; recoverable errors are values (`result` with polymorphic variants) that Eta's combinators are made aware of through an error mode. Combinators throw exactly what the body would throw, add nothing, swallow nothing, and only suppress interruptions onto the primary cause.

Consequences: `Concurrent` is rewritten as thin wrappers over `Eio.Fiber`/`Switch` (the bugs of 2026-09-28 were in Eta's own switch management, not Eio's); a `supervised_error` scope carries typed errors across forks by value, which is the direct-style answer to typed errors in concurrency — `handled_effect`'s local token cannot cross an Eio fork and we stop looking for a way around that; `retry` gains Ox's `ResultPolicy`; `Flow` (Ox's lazy direct-style stream) is the model for streams when an application pulls them; `RateLimiter`, `select`, a typed `Actor` and `OxApp` (our Door) complete the floor. Rejected as before: an interpreter, a dependency-injection layer, effect-row typing (unavailable in OCaml; Kyo and OxCaml modes are watched). The v1 reason for dropping object capabilities — objects are not `portable` across OxCaml domains — is recorded as a single-domain caveat, not a blocker, since no consumer crosses domains.
